I am a PhD student in the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Aditi Raghunathan, and supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

I study how to train models which are as easy as possible to continue training.

About

I'm excited about solving mysteries in machine learning. I'm broadly interested in the science of foundation models, with current work focused on optimization, robustness, and inference-time methods. Lately I've been thinking about how to train models that, by design, can be fine-tuned easily and robustly to new tasks, and how optimization shapes that.

I did my undergrad at Swarthmore College, and have spent time at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, MIT, Los Alamos National Laboratory, and Apple, where I worked with many lovely people. Please reach out if you'd like to chat — I love talking about research.
